# Data card: `spectral-txn-risk` (synthetic)

- **What:** 40,000 card transactions from a seeded generator (`src/spectral_canary/data.py`,
  `GENERATOR_VERSION = txn-risk-gen-1.0`, seed 20261108). **No real people, cards or payments.**
- **License:** MIT, generated by this repository's code. Provenance and hashes are in `manifests/dataset_manifest.json`.
- **Label:** `is_fraud = 1` means the transaction was later confirmed as a fraudulent chargeback. It is drawn
  as Bernoulli(sigmoid(z)) with the documented risk function `_risk_logit`:
  log-amount, card-not-present, country mismatch, device trust (plus a step below 0.3), night hours, 24h
  velocity, account age, distance, merchant category, and three non-linear interactions (card-not-present ×
  amount > $400, night × gift cards, high velocity × new account).
- **Positive rate:** about 6.2% overall (exact counts per split in the manifest).
- **Split:** a seeded permutation by row id: 60% train / 20% validation / 20% test. Tests assert the splits are
  disjoint, together cover every row, and share no duplicate feature rows. The test split is frozen: nothing is
  fit or tuned on it.
- **Reproducibility:** regenerating the dataset must give the committed `dataset_hash`
  (`test_dataset_hash_matches_committed_manifest`). The Docker build regenerates it on Linux and checks the
  same hash, along with every model's test-prediction hash.
- **Shift scenario** `high_value_online_surge` (SIMULATED): a different merchant mix (more online,
  electronics and gift cards), amounts × e^1.4, card-present probability × 0.35, lower device trust. The fraud
  rate rises to 41.4% (measured on 20,000 seeded rows).

## Why synthetic instead of a public benchmark

Known ground truth means the simulated production traffic can deliver **delayed labels**. That is what lets
the canary make (and refuse to make) labeled quality claims, and a static benchmark can't do it. Synthetic
data also carries no licensing, privacy or PII constraints. The cost is external validity, which
`DRIFT_AND_LIMITATIONS.md` states directly.
